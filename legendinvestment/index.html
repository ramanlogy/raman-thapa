Your current website, honestly.

01

Google sees an empty page

The site arrives as a blank shell and draws everything with JavaScript afterwards. Search engines and link previews read only the title.

02

Two addresses, one site

legendsinvestment.com.np and legendsproperty.com.np show the same pages. Google treats that as duplicate content and splits your ranking.

03

Numbers that disagree

Rikhetar's text says 90% of plots are sold, while its live counter shows 0 sold of 30. Sipadol shows the same gap. A careful buyer notices.

04

Small typos, big signal

"Real State" in the search keywords and "Secutiry" on the Sipadol cover image. Easy fixes that protect a premium brand.

05

No share preview

No preview image or title is set, so a link shared on Facebook, Viber or WhatsApp shows up as plain text.

06

Great material, hidden away

Drone videos, plot maps and the Naya Gaon renders exist, but sit inside PDFs and 24 MB video files most phones will not load.
