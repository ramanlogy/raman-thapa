## 6. Website audit: improvement opportunities for the proposal

**Technical / SEO**
1. **Google sees an empty page.** The site is a client-rendered Vue SPA, so the HTML Google receives contains only `<div id="app">`. Projects, prices and blogs are invisible to search crawlers and to link previews on WhatsApp and Facebook. Fix: SSR or static pre-rendering (Nuxt/Astro), per-page titles and meta tags, OG images, and schema.org `RealEstateListing`/`Place` markup.
2. **The JS bundle is about 2.5 MB** and the public and admin panels ship in one file. That is slow on Nepali mobile data. Fix: split the admin code out and lazy-load routes.
3. **The project cover images are only 344×200 px**, so they look blurry on any modern screen. The header logo is 80×80 px.
4. **Several images are AI-generated** (blog covers are named "ChatGPT Image…" and "Leonardo Phoenix…"). Swapping in real drone and site photos would build more trust.
5. **Inventory data is stale or inconsistent.** Palanse and Sipadol are marketed as "90% sold", yet the site shows 0 sold and all plots open. Chalnakhel blog maps show sold plots, but the site lists all 69 as open. Buyers notice this kind of mismatch.
6. **Contact and brand inconsistencies:** the displayed email (info@) opens a Gmail address; two domains serve the same site; the Instagram bio link is broken; the brand name switches between Investment, Property, Properties and Group; the footer still says ©2023.
7. Two "Bank" slides duplicate each other, and the homepage is carousel-heavy.

**Content / trust gaps (your biggest wins)**
8. **No About / Leadership page.** The MD, chief engineer and team are not shown anywhere on the site. Add a team section: MD message from Rabin Chamling Rai, Chief Engineer Dinesh Pokharel, and the advisors.
9. **The ambassadors are underused.** Vijay Lama and Rekha Thapa, their best-performing assets, appear only in one slider image. Give them a dedicated section with an embedded video.
10. **No testimonials section**, even though they have video testimonials (Mina Thapa, Sangita Silwal Ayer, Mrs Adhikari, Prof. Bam Thapa).
11. **Partners appear only as a slider image.** Add a proper logo wall for the four banks and Subisu, plus an EMI/loan calculator built around those banks.
12. **No legal/trust centre.** Their own blog stresses Lalpurja verification, KVDA permits and AML compliance. Turn that into downloadable permits per project (they already hold the PDFs).
13. **Nepali language toggle**, aimed at the 37–60 audience and NRNs.
14. **Interactive plot map:** they already have plot-level data (lot no., facing, area, price, status). Replace the static colour-coded maps with a clickable, live-status plot picker plus WhatsApp booking.
15. **NRN funnel:** add time-zone-friendly virtual site-visit booking and video walkthroughs (the project videos already exist).
16. **Events / Impact page:** collect the plantations, bank orientations, Youth Festival sponsorship and the Subisu smart-community MOU in one place.

## 7. ⚠️ Sensitivities: read before pitching

- **Nepali Times, "Plotting to plunder" (Archana Darji, 31 May 2025):** the article alleges that **Nic Vujicic Housing** (co-owned by Purna Bahadur Limbu and Prabin Kusma) cleared community forest at Ghising Danda without a KVDA/municipal planning permit and evicted residents. Legends Property is not named in the article. **Do not reference this in the proposal.** It does explain why a strong "Legal & Trust Centre" (permits, KVDA approvals) would be valuable to them.
- If Six Sigma Inc. (whose chairman shares the MD's name) built the current site, frame the proposal as an "upgrade / next phase", not as "your site is bad".
- Use the ambassador photos and video thumbnails in a **private** pitch mock-up only. They are the client's licensed brand assets.
