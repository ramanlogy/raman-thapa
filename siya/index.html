6. Website improvements for the proposal

Technical / SEO

  1. Google sees an empty page. The site is client-rendered Vue, so projects and blogs are invisible to search engines and to WhatsApp/Facebook link previews. Fix: pre-render or SSR, plus meta and OG tags and schema markup.
  2. The JS bundle is about 2.5 MB, with the admin panel shipped to every visitor. That is slow on mobile data.
  3. Project cover images are only 344×200 px, so they look blurry. The logo is 80×80.
  4. Many images are AI-generated (ChatGPT / Leonardo). Real drone and site photos would build more trust.
  5. Plot status doesn't match the marketing. Palanse and Sipadol are marketed as "90% sold", but the site shows 0 sold.
  6. Inconsistent identity: the email links to Gmail, two domains serve the same site, the Instagram link is broken, the brand name varies, and the footer still says ©2023.

Content & trust (biggest wins)

  1. No About / Leadership page. Add an MD message, the Chief Engineer and the team.
  2. The ambassadors are underused, appearing only in one slider. Give them a dedicated section with video.
  3. No testimonials section, even though many video testimonials exist.
  4. Partner logo wall plus an EMI / loan calculator built around the four banks.
  5. Legal / trust centre: per-project permits, KVDA approval and Lalpurja process. They already have the PDFs.
  6. Nepali language toggle for buyers aged 37–60.
  7. Interactive plot map: clickable live-status plots (they already have lot, facing, area and price data) with WhatsApp booking.
  8. NRN funnel: virtual site visits and video walkthroughs.
  9. Events / Impact page: plantations, bank orientations, the Youth Festival and the Subisu smart-community MOU.

7. ⚠️ Be careful when pitching

  • A Nepali Times article ("Plotting to plunder", 31 May 2025) alleges that partner developer Nic Vujicic Housing cleared forest at Ghising Danda without a permit and evicted residents. Legends isn't named in it. Never mention it. It is, however, a reason why a strong legal / trust section would help them.
  • The Six Sigma Inc. chairman shares the MD's name, and Six Sigma may have built the current site. Pitch your work as an "upgrade / next phase", not "your site is bad".
  • The ambassador photos and video stills are the client's brand assets. Use them in the private pitch only.
