How I work

Every project follows the same simple process.

  1. 01 Understand I learn about your business, your customers, and what the site actually needs to do.
  2. 02 Design I sketch a layout that fits your brand, not a copy-pasted template.
  3. 03 Build I code it to be fast and work well on every phone and screen size.
  4. 04 Launch & support I publish it, connect your domain, and stick around for changes after.
